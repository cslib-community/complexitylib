# Goldreich draft exercise inventory

Audit: Christian Reitwiessner, with Claude Code assistance.

This inventory classifies the end-of-chapter exercises of Oded Goldreich's draft revisions of
*Computational Complexity: A Conceptual Perspective* (December 2006 to May 2007, from the
[author's drafts page](https://www.wisdom.weizmann.ac.il/~oded/cc-drafts.html); file names and
SHA-256 digests are listed in [#4772](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/4772)),
the source of the [chapter audit](goldreich-2007.md). Christian Reitwiessner's scope includes
exercises that establish new theory; routine practice is not a formalization target. Numbers and
pages belong to the drafts, not the 2008 published edition.

Coverage of the chapters:

- Chapter 1 and Appendices A–G (`r6.pdf`) have no exercise sections.
- Chapters 2 and 5–10 are classified below. Chapter 7 uses `r4.pdf` (March 2007), as the chapter
  audit does, not the earlier `r3.pdf` copy. Printed page = PDF page + offset: `r1.pdf` −25,
  `r2.pdf` +116, `r3.pdf` +202, `r4.pdf` +264, `r5.pdf` +382.
- Chapter 3's twelve exercises are inventoried in open PR
  [#4852](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/pull/4852), which also proves the
  Exercise 3.12 endpoint. They are not repeated here.
- Chapter 4's twelve exercises are inventoried in the
  [#4772 Chapter 4 pass](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/4772#issuecomment-6841285129).
  Exercises 4.3 and 4.5 are covered in the chapter audit
  ([4.3](goldreich-2007.md#theory-exercise-43-sublinear-time),
  [4.5](goldreich-2007.md#chapter-4-exact-step-counter-exercise)); 4.6–4.7 are claimed by
  [#5086](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/5086) with PR
  [#5111](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/pull/5111).

Each exercise, or each subpart where subparts differ, has one class:

- **T**: establishes new theory: a named theorem, a class relation, a construction or bound reused
  later, a characterization, or a step the main text delegates to the exercise. **Partial** marks
  library results that cover part of the endpoint; the row says what remains.
- **C**: covered by the cited library declarations in a model the chapter audit accepts, with
  the stated qualifications.
- **S**: source specification needed or source issue: an undetermined or false statement. Where an
  evident repair exists, the row gives it as a **corrected endpoint**. Typos confined to a
  guideline are noted in T rows instead.
- **R**: routine practice, listed in one line per chapter.

## Chapter 2 exercises

Source: `r1.pdf`, PDF pp. 130–139 / printed pp. 105–114 (printed = PDF − 25, checked against the
